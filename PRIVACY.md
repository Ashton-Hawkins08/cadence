# Privacy Policy for Cadence

**Last updated: September 2026**

Cadence ("the app") is developed by Ashton Hawkins. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's handled.

## Summary

Cadence does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All of your practice data stays on your own device.

## What data the app uses, and where it stays

- **Practice data** (categories, exercises, session logs, BPM history, calendar events, notes) is stored locally in a database on your device only. It is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere.
- **Microphone audio** (used by the chromatic tuner and Tempo Ear features) is processed entirely on-device, in real time, to detect pitch and rhythm. Audio is never recorded to a file, never stored, and never sent off your device in any form.
- **Settings** (your name, instrument, theme preference, and reminder settings) are stored locally on your device only.

## Data we do NOT collect

- No account creation, no sign-in, no personal identifiers
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising identifiers
- No crash reporting services that transmit data off-device
- No location data
- No contacts, photos, or files other than images you explicitly choose to import for your own sheet music

## Permissions the app requests, and why

- **Microphone** — required for the chromatic tuner and Tempo Ear (rhythm detection) features. Audio is processed on-device only, as described above.
- **Notifications** — used to show a persistent notification while the metronome is actively running in the background, and to remind you about upcoming practice.
- **Storage/photos access** — used only when you choose to import an image of sheet music into the app. Nothing is uploaded.

## Children's privacy

Cadence does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no data at all, there is no personal information to protect or disclose for users of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
